How it works

One structured order, start to finish

Four steps, about five minutes. You can stop after any of them — the order saves as a draft.

1
Pick a laboratory
Browse approved labs by city, service and turnaround. Every price is published up front.
2
Fill the lab's own form
Teeth on a map, shade from a picker, material from a list. Required fields can't be skipped.
3
Attach scans, set the due date
Digital impressions up to 100 MB per file, a requested due date, and rush if the lab offers it.
4
Track it to delivery
Confirmed price and due date from the lab, a status on every step, and the invoice at the end.

Questions

What does it cost?expand_more
Creating an account is free for doctors, clinics and laboratories. Each laboratory sets its own service prices, and the order shows them before you submit.
Do you support physical impressions?expand_more
At this stage we work with digital impressions only — you attach scan files to the order. Conventional-impression workflows are on the roadmap.
How does a laboratory get listed?expand_more
Register, complete your legal and billing details, then submit for review. Once approved you appear in the doctors' lab directory and can publish services.
Can I change an order after sending it?expand_more
Yes, until the case is closed. You pick a reason for the change, the lab sees exactly what changed, and confirms whether the price or due date moves.
Which languages are supported?expand_more
English, Georgian and Russian, switchable anywhere in the app — including the order forms your lab publishes.
